You asked an AI to build you a quote calculator. It works. It saves you twenty minutes every time you use it. And right now it's sitting in your Downloads folder where nobody else can reach it.

This is the single most common story we hear. So here's the fix, start to finish.

## Step 1 — Build with any AI

Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable — whatever you like. Ask it for a tool and you'll get an HTML file. You don't need to know how the code works; you just need it to run.

## Step 2 — Drag it onto AppHub

Open your team's dashboard and drag the file onto the upload zone. Give it a name and pick an icon. If it isn't plain HTML — JSX, a Vue component, a zip, raw model output — AppHub converts it automatically.

## Step 3 — It appears in the grid

Your tool shows up instantly in the phone-style grid, right next to everyone else's tools. No deployment pipeline, no waiting for a ticket to clear.

## Step 4 — Your team uses it

They click the icon and the tool opens in a secure sandboxed viewer. No accounts to create, no installs, no training session.

That's the whole loop. The hard part was never the building — AI made that easy. The hard part was the last thirty seconds, and that's exactly the part AppHub removes.
