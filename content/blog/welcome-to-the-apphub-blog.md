We started AppHub with a simple belief: the best tools are built by the people who feel the pain. This blog is where we'll share what we're learning as teams around the world turn that belief into a daily habit.

## What you'll find here

Expect three kinds of writing:

- **Product updates** — the new capabilities we ship, and how to get the most out of them.
- **Playbooks** — practical guides for building, sharing, and running tools with your team.
- **Stories** — real examples of small tools that changed how a team works.

> The portal is the surface. The culture shift is the product.

## Why a blog, and why now

As AppHub has grown from a simple tool portal into a collaborative platform — real-time collaboration, a prompt library, a marketplace, version history — we've collected a lot of ideas that don't fit on a landing page. This is where they'll live.

If there's something you'd like us to write about, we'd love to hear it. In the meantime, thanks for reading — and happy building.
